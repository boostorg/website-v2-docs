== Libraries Consumed into the Standard

The following libraries, in whole or in a significant part, are now part of the pass:[C++] Standard. If you are considering using any one of these libraries, it would be good practice to investigate the Standard equivalents before deciding whether to include any of these, as the functionality you are looking for may already be available.

[cols="1,1,1,1,1,2,2",stripes=even,options="header",frame=none]
|===
| Library | Minimum pass:[C++] | Consumed by Standard | Standard version | Header-only | Status | Status Notes
| Algorithm | pass:[C++]03 | Partly | pass:[C++]20+ | Yes | Active | Generic algorithms; some overlap with standard algorithms
| Align | pass:[C++]03 | Partly | pass:[C++]11+ | Yes | Maintained | Alignment facilities largely standardized
| Any | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | pass:[C++]17 | Yes | Maintained | std::any is standard; Boost version remains useful for compatibility
| Array | pass:[C++]03 | Yes | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | Maintained | std::array supersedes principal use
| Atomic | pass:[C++]11 | Partly | pass:[C++]11 | *No* | Active | Boost adds capabilities beyond std::atomic
| Bind | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | Legacy / Maintained | Largely superseded by lambdas and std::bind
| CharConv | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | pass:[C++]17 | Yes | Active | Character conversion; compatibility and extensions
| Chrono | pass:[C++]11 | Partly | pass:[C++]11 | *No* | Maintained | std::chrono covers core functionality, Chrono adds clocks and utilities
| Compat | pass:[C++]11 | Yes | Various | Yes | Active | Compatibility implementations for newer standard facilities
