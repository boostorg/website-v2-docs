* boost_phrase:library[Asio,/libs/asio/]
** Added `eager`, a completion token that starts an asynchronous operation immediately and allows its result to be waited on later.
** Added `trampoline<>` and `make_trampoline()`, an executor adapter that runs nested submissions in a bounded thread-local queue, rather than resubmitting them to the target executor.
** Added `execution::staging_executor` and `execution::stage()`, to reduce the cost of `require`/`prefer` chains on polymorphic executors.
** Added overloads of `cancel_after` that cancel an operation when a "trigger" asynchronous operation completes.
** Added support for sending and receiving multiple datagrams in a single call.
** Promoted `channel` and `concurrent_channel` from the `experimental` namespace, added `can_send` and `can_receive` to the channel
classes, and added `try_peek` to `basic_channel`.
** Added an optional "spin" reactor backend that avoids demultiplexing system calls such as `epoll_wait` by polling descriptors directly.
** Added the `descriptor_name` I/O control command, for per-descriptor configuration.
** Added a Perfetto-format handler tracking backend.
** Added a dependency-free `spawn()` implementation for Linux and macOS on x86-64 and AArch64, which may be explicitly enabled.
** Added `awaitable` to the forward declarations in `boost/asio/fwd.hpp`.
** Added support for `final` allocator types to the `io_context` executor.
** Added an associated executor to `experimental::promise`pass:['s] wait operation.
** Changed custom handler tracking so that `BOOST_ASIO_HANDLER_CREATION` may be called without the execution context argument.
** Changed `poll()` to bound the amount of work performed per call.
** Optimised the scheduler, coroutines, completion handling, polymorphic executors, the reactor and the recycling allocator, and reduced the size of the `thread_pool` executor.
** Optimised `read_until` and `async_read_until` when used with a character delimiter.
** Fixed a potential deadlock in the reactor when completing operations during shutdown or after a descriptor error.
** Fixed a use-after-free when destroying a nested `any_completion_handler`.
** Fixed `cancel_after` and `cancel_at` with reentrant cancellation.
** Fixed `spawn()` when the coroutine completes immediately.
** Fixed pending channel sends being lost on close, and `try_send*` when used with multiple signatures.
** Fixed the associator binder and work dispatcher move constructors.
** Fixed duplicate work counting with a polymorphic I/O executor.
** Fixed the `async_connect` continuation hooks.
** Fixed `experimental::ranged_parallel_group` with operations that have no associated executor.
** Fixed IOCP wake-up packet accumulation, `stream_file` position accounting, and 4 GiB buffer truncation on Windows.
** Fixed iteration over empty resolver results.
** Fixed `multicast::enable_loopback` for IPv6 on Solaris and illumos.
** Fixed thread sanitizer support on older toolchains.
** Fixed compilation without `SA_RESTART` or with bounds safety attributes enabled.
** Fixed warnings on clang-cl, OpenBSD and illumos.
** Consult the boost_at:/doc/libs/1_93_0/doc/html/boost_asio/history.html[Revision History] for further details.

* boost_phrase:library[Decimal,/libs/decimal/]:
** Breaking changes:
*** `rescale(val, precision)` now rounds `val` to `precision` digits after the decimal point using the current rounding mode, and a negative `precision` rounds to the corresponding power of ten. With the default `precision` of 0 it now rounds to an integer instead of matching `trunc`.
*** The quantum operations `quantexp`, `samequantum`, and `quantize` are now a compile error for the fast types, since they normalize on construction and have no cohorts. Use `rescale` to round any type to a fixed number of fractional digits.
*** `logb` and `ilogb` now return the unbiased exponent as IEEE 754 requires (e.g. `logb(1E-3)` is now `-3`).
** New features:
*** Added `from_binary`, which rounds the exact value of a `float` or `double` to a decimal type in the current rounding mode (IEEE 754 convertFormat). The constructors keep the shortest representation instead.
*** Added `chars_format::cohort_preserving_fixed` to `to_chars` and `from_chars`. In both cohort preserving modes, `from_chars` now rejects input whose exact cohort cannot be stored instead of silently underflowing to zero or overflowing to infinity.
*** Added `roundeven`.
*** Added NATVIS support for the MSVC debugger, and made the LLDB and GDB pretty printers display values the same way.
*** Converting `float` and `double` to a decimal type now uses Dragonbox, which is about 17 times faster for `double` and 12 times faster for `float`, and fixes the last digit for some values.
*** Sped up addition, multiplication, and division.
** Bug fixes:
*** Fixed and modernized the {cpp}20 module (`import boost.decimal;`) to match the other Boost libraries, and added module testing with Clang, GCC, and MSVC.
*** Fixed incorrect rounding in many edge cases, several found by greatly expanded decTest coverage: subnormal products and quotients, sums of operands with very different exponents, quotients and exact cancellations in non-default rounding modes, overflow in modes that round to the largest finite value, and values below the smallest subnormal.
*** Fixed `fma` losing the addend for `decimal32_t` and `decimal64_t` when the product's exponent is much smaller than the addend's, and made `fma` for the 128-bit types round only once.
*** Fixed `operator==` returning `false` for zeros with different exponents, hashing so that all zeros hash equally, and hashing on 32-bit platforms.
*** Fixed construction of subnormals and of values whose exponent is out of range but whose significand has room to absorb it.
*** Fixed the sign of the infinity from dividing a negative value by zero, and `decimal32_t` comparison of two positive infinities.
*** Fixed `quantexp` returning the wrong value, and `comparetotal` between fast and non-fast types.
*** Fixed `remainder` with non-finite values, `fdim` for an infinite `x` and for a signaling NaN, and `acosh` and `atanh` to return NaN outside of their domains.
*** Fixed parsing to round using all the input digits rather than only the first 19 (38 for `decimal128_t`), and to keep the zeros after the decimal point when the exponent is zero.
*** Fixed conversion to binary floating point dropping the sign of zero and infinity.
*** Improved the accuracy and monotonicity of `log`, `log10`, `log2`, `log1p`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `atan2`, and the inverse hyperbolic functions, made `sqrt` correctly rounded in the current rounding mode, and made `cbrt` round only once.
*** `ceil`, `floor`, `round`, `roundeven`, `trunc`, `rint`, `lrint`, `lround`, `llround`, `frexp`, `ldexp`, `scalbn`, `scalbln`, `fmod`, `remainder`, and `remquo` now operate directly on the integer significand and exponent, so their results are exact.

* boost_phrase:library[Math,/libs/math/]:
** Breaking changes:
*** `double` arguments are no longer promoted to `long double` internally by default, as `BOOST_MATH_PROMOTE_DOUBLE_POLICY` now defaults to `false` on all platforms.
    This is much faster where `long double` is x87 or software emulated (e.g. AArch64 Linux), but results for `double` may differ in the last few bits.
    To restore the old behavior define `BOOST_MATH_PROMOTE_DOUBLE_POLICY` as `true`, or pass `promote_double<true>` in a policy (boost_gh:issue[math,1211]).
*** The quantiles of discrete distributions now default to `integer_round_up` instead of `integer_round_outwards`, matching R, SciPy, Julia and Mathematica,
    so lower quantiles are often one larger than before. The geometric distribution now also rounds its quantile as the policy asks.
    To restore the old behavior define `BOOST_MATH_DISCRETE_QUANTILE_POLICY` as `integer_round_outwards`, or pass `discrete_quantile<integer_round_outwards>` in a policy (boost_gh:issue[math,1203]).
*** `hypergeometric_pFq` now raises an evaluation error when most of the digits of the result are lost to cancellation, instead of silently returning an inaccurate value.
*** `newton_raphson_iterate`, `halley_iterate` and `schroder_iterate` now raise an evaluation error when they walk onto a bound that is not a root, instead of returning the bound.
** New features:
*** Added support for consuming the library as a {cpp}20 module with `import boost.math;`.
*** Added a user configurable top-level namespace with `BOOST_MATH_NAMESPACE_COMPONENTS`, so that libraries embedding their own copy of Boost.Math can avoid conflicts.
*** Added prime number functions `prime_sieve`, `prime_range` and `prime_count`, backed by a segmented sieve with optional multithreaded and CUDA execution.
*** Added the von Mises and generalized extreme value distributions.
*** Added Gauss-Hermite quadrature, Van den Bos quadrature over the unit square, and symplectic integrators for Hamiltonian systems (`integrate_hamiltonian`, with adaptive step size and cubic Hermite interpolation variants).
*** Added a fast Fourier transform (`tools::fft` and `tools::real_fft`) for any complex type.
    `cardinal_trigonometric` and `chebyshev_transform` now use it and no longer require FFTW.
*** Added polynomial root finders `polynomial_roots` and `polynomial_roots_companion_matrix`.
*** Added `lbeta`, `libeta` and `libetac` (the logarithms of `beta`, `ibeta` and `ibetac`), and `ligamma` and `ligamma_lower` (the logarithms of `tgamma(a, x)` and `tgamma_lower(a, x)`).
    `logcdf` and its complement now use the logarithmic incomplete beta and gamma functions, so they stay finite and accurate in the tails for many distributions.
*** Added `pow1p`, and a three argument overload of `ccmath::hypot`.
*** Added `entropy` for the remaining distributions, except the non-central ones.
*** Added degrees of freedom finders `find_v1` and `find_v2` to the Fisher F distribution, and improved the robustness of the non-central F finders.
*** Added the M nearest neighbour Chatterjee correlation, `chatterjee_correlation_mnn`.
*** Added `ulps_heatmap` for plotting the accuracy of functions of two variables.
*** Added support for vector and matrix valued integrands (e.g. Eigen types) to the quadrature routines, via overloads taking an explicit zero and norm.
*** Added policy based error handling to the interpolators.
*** Added hexfloat support to `__float128` stream I/O.
*** Improved the speed of `float_distance` for IEEE `float`, `double` and binary128, and made `ellint_rj` about 1.7 times faster.
*** Improved the accuracy and speed of the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distribution.
*** Deprecated the `polynomial` constructor taking a pointer and an order.
** Bug fixes:
*** Improved the accuracy of `ibeta` and `ibeta_inv` for small parameters, near the mean, and when the binomial sum underflows, fixed the arcsine special case, and kept `ibeta` monotonic when the compiler contracts to FMA.
*** Fixed `lgamma_p` where P underflows, the accuracy of `gamma_p` for small `a` with `x < a`, and the accuracy of `tgamma1pm1` for multiprecision types.
*** Fixed the non-central t distribution: cancellation in the left tail and for small degrees of freedom, and its degrees of freedom domain checking.
*** Fixed the non-central F and beta CDFs for huge denominator degrees of freedom, and the non-central beta CDF and its complement not summing to one.
*** Fixed the Student's t quantile for very small probabilities and its accuracy for non-integer degrees of freedom between 2 and 3.
*** Fixed overflow and cancellation in the inverse Gaussian CDFs, cancellation in the skew normal tails, cancellation in the Poisson `logpdf` for large arguments, and swapped location and scale in the Laplace `logpdf`.
*** Fixed the hypergeometric PDF for non-integer `x`, and its accuracy for population sizes above 170.
*** Fixed discrete quantiles to round trip with the CDF, and a hang for values beyond 1/epsilon.
*** Fixed the Airy functions near zero, `heuman_lambda`, `jacobi_dn`, the incomplete `ellint_2`, `expint`, Laguerre polynomials, `cyl_bessel_j` for integer orders, underflow in `cyl_bessel_k`, and hangs and precision loss in the Jacobi theta functions.
*** Fixed a hang in `hypergeometric_1F1`, and accuracy issues in `hypergeometric_1F1`, `hypergeometric_0F1` and `hypergeometric_2F0`.
*** Fixed `logcdf` and its complement to send an exact negative infinity to the overflow error policy.
*** Fixed `std::complex<__float128>` `pow` for a negative real and zero imaginary part, and the root finders accepting NaN as a bound.
*** Fixed `mode` assertions and sorting, `mean` silently giving the wrong answer for input iterators (now a `static_assert`), and the second variance accumulator in the parallel correlation.
*** Fixed `polynomial<int> % scalar` returning zero, `simple_continued_fraction` printing, assignment and canonical form, `fvar` being implicitly convertible from any type, and Bezier async evaluation.
*** Fixed pchip integration with a circular buffer, Daubechies wavelet derivative scaling, and the Whittaker-Shannon interpolant at higher precision.
*** Fixed uninitialized reads in complex range checks and `evaluation_condition_number`, and made implicit integer to `Real` conversions explicit.
